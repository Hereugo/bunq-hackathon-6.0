# Goal

Goal: An AI Assistant which helps our user in budget decisions.

# User Stories

## Story 1: Effortless Task Execution via Natural Language

As a busy bank customer,
I want to ask the AI assistant for what I need in plain language (like “I need last month’s bank statement” or “Cancel my Spotify subscription”),
So that the assistant can understand my request and immediately take action without me navigating through menus or forms.

**Scenario:**
When I need a specific document, want to manage a subscription, or request account info, I just tell the assistant what I need. It interprets my intent and handles the task — fetching, canceling, or updating as needed — with minimal follow-up.

**Pain Point Addressed:**
Users waste time and energy trying to locate the right section or perform tasks manually in complex banking apps.

**AI-Powered Benefit:**
The assistant acts as a personal banker, turning natural requests into real actions instantly, reducing friction and boosting efficiency.

## Story 2: Natural Language Settings Management

As a user who finds navigating app settings confusing or time-consuming,
I want to simply describe what I want to change in plain language,
So that the AI assistant can adjust my settings for me or guide me directly to the right option.

**Scenario:**
Instead of searching through a complicated settings menu, I can type or say something like “Turn off overdraft notifications” or “Change my payment limit to $500.” The AI interprets my request and either makes the change or brings me directly to the setting I need.

**Pain Point Addressed:**
Traditional settings menus can be overwhelming and unintuitive, leading to user frustration.

**AI-Powered Benefit:**
The assistant simplifies customization by acting as a smart layer between the user and the settings system, reducing friction and saving time.



## Story 3: Budget-Conscious Aid in Decision Making

As a budget-conscious bank customer, 
I want an AI assistant that can help me offer financial options based on a described situation and relevant variables, 
so that I can make informed choices and stay within my budget.

**Scenario:**
When I’m considering dining out, I can show or describe the menu to the AI assistant and my given budget. The AI scans the menu, estimates the cost of my meal, evaluates it against my current budget and spending patterns, and gives me an offer of what I could allow myself to order.

**Pain Point Addressed:**
Users struggle to evaluate the financial impact of real-life decisions in dynamic, context-specific situations, leading to overspending or missed savings opportunities.

**AI-Powered Benefit:**
The AI processes user-described scenarios, interprets key financial variables, and provides personalized, budget-conscious recommendations in real time to support better decision-making.

## Story 4: Real-Time Budget Impact Alerts

As a budget-conscious bank customer,
I want my AI assistant to automatically warn me about the budget impact of a transaction before I complete it,
So that I can make informed choices and stay within my budget.

**Scenario:**
Whenever I am about to make a potential purchase (e.g., online checkout, point-of-sale scan, or manual entry), the AI instantly calculates how it affects my weekly/monthly budget and alerts me if it would cause overspending.

**Pain Point Addressed:**
Users often overspend because they lack real-time feedback on how a transaction fits into their budget.

**AI-Powered Benefit:**
The assistant acts as a financial guardrail, offering immediate insight and suggesting alternatives if the purchase isn't budget-friendly.

## Story 5: Shared Expense Handling via Receipt Photo

As a user who frequently splits bills with friends,
I want to take a photo of a receipt and have the AI assistant automatically calculate and send my friend their share,
So that I can easily manage shared expenses without doing manual calculations or reminders.

**Scenario:**
After dining with a friend, I snap a photo of the check. The AI analyzes the receipt, determines the fair split (or allows me to customize it), and sends a payment request or bill to my friend through their preferred payment method or banking app.

**Pain Point Addressed:**
Manually splitting bills and reminding friends to pay can be awkward and time-consuming.

**AI-Powered Benefit:**
The assistant automates expense sharing, making social spending seamless and reducing friction in personal finance management.
